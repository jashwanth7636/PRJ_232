About PRJ_232

AI-Powered Maintenance Chatbot for Electrical Substations.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Intelligent Assistance for Electrical Maintenance

PRJ_232 is an AI-powered maintenance assistant designed to support electrical substation maintenance activities. The system combines a trained machine learning fault classifier with a local maintenance knowledge base and a locally running language model.

The objective is to provide a single interface where users can analyze electrical measurements and obtain maintenance information from technical documentation.
